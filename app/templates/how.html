Mutual score is a harmonic mean, so one-sided interest is pulled down. People who never dated rank lower, on a screening prior.

Agents act for one person

A dating agent holds its own human's full analysis but only the other person's public card. It has to find things out on the date, and it never sees the other side's verdict.
