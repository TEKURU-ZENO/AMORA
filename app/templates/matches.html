Stable Matching · Irving's Algorithm

AMORA implements Irving's (1985) algorithm for the Stable Roommates Problem across the single unpartitioned dating pool. When a stable matching exists, it guarantees zero blocking pairs: no two participants would mutually prefer to leave their assigned matches for each other. If cyclical preference deadlocks prevent stability, the algorithm honestly reports that no stable matching exists.
